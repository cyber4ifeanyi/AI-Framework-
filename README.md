# AI-Framework-
The four concepts of Al Fluency - The 4D Framework 
Apply the 4D's
How to apply one of these collaboration scenarios of the 4D Framework for a marketing email campaign scenario
Delegation : 
Handle myself : I will personally handle the high stakes stuff, final approvals, brand voice sign-offs, and anything touching pricing, legal, or compliance. I will also step in whenever we need real world context, AI just can't pick up on, like past campaign missteps, sales feedback, or direct customer complaints.
Collaborate with AI : I will use AI as a collaborator to knock out first drafts, brainstorm subject lines, and generate options for A/B testing. It's also great for tightening up rough copy and giving us a bunch of different tones to react to before we lock anything in
Description :
Give AI concrete inputs, not vibes: target audience (who are they, what do they care about), the specific offer/CTA, tone (e.g., "confident but not salesy," "warm, like a friend recommending something"), examples of past emails that worked or didn't, and what "success" looks like (opens, clicks, conversions, or just "doesn't sound like every other promo email")
Give constraints too: word count, banned phrases, required disclaimers, brand style guide if one exists.
Discernment :
Does it actually sound like the brand, or generic AI-marketing-speak?
Are claims accurate and defensible (nothing overstated or fabricated)?
Does the CTA match the actual campaign goal?
Would this email make me want to open the next one, or does it feel like spam?
Check for hallucinated details — stats, product features, dates — that need verification
Diligence :
Disclose internally (and possibly externally, depending on company policy) that AI was used in drafting
Don't let AI have the final say on anything customer-facing without a human review pass
Be accountable for the output as if you wrote it yourself — "the AI wrote it" isn't a valid excuse if something goes wrong
Watch for AI reinforcing bias in targeting language or making assumptions about the audience that need a human sanity check.
